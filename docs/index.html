A tiny friend that lives in your notch

MoMo watches your AI coding agents — Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and more — lets you approve Claude Code's requests from the notch, chat with Anthropic, Google AI or OpenAI, and keeps an eye on Stripe, GitHub, Vercel, n8n and more. Free and open source.
